# Roadmap

What we plan to add to Reflipped, grouped by theme. Items are listed roughly in the order we expect to get to them, but there are no dates: this is a hobby project. For what already works, see the [README](../README.md#what-works).

## Accounts
Today your progress is tied to the app on your phone: reinstalling it, or switching phones, starts a new player unless the server owner moves your save by hand.
- **Player accounts** on a website run by the server, with a username and password (and optional two-factor sign-in).
- **Signing in from the app**, so a new install picks up your account.
- **Linking a device** to your account with a short one-time code, so your save follows you to a new phone.
- **Lost device**: remove a phone from your account from the website.
- **Your save on the website**: download your own save, and other per-player options.
- Existing players can claim the progress they made before accounts existed.

## Everyday play
- **Login bonuses**: the daily login calendar and its rewards.
- **Missions**: the last kinds of mission progress (items and mana collected, stamina used, and a few others), repeatable missions, and titles from events and rankings.
- **Mail**: daily challenge and reward point mail, the mail history, and sending mail to every player at once.

## Game modes and events
- **Event calendar**: the server owner picks which events are live, instead of only moving the server's date.
- **The Haunt**: the monthly 30-floor tower.
- **Solo Boss Challenges**: the hardest solo boss fights, with their daily clear limit.
- **Treasure Zone**: entry with Golden Skulls.
- **The Trove**: the daily mana shop with its rotating stock.
- **Exchange shops**: every Boss Coin, event item and Star Sliver/Speck exchange and the Trading Post, checked end to end.
- **Other event types**: Score Scramble, Speed Runs and Chrono Trials, Carnival and World Story.
- **Raid events** such as Sagas of Conquest and Necropolis.
- **Portals**: the remaining portal rules, such as pity and selectable rewards, and box gacha resets.
- **Practice Combat and skill previews.**

## Co-op
- **Room links**, finished and tested, so you can share a room with friends.
- **Keeping your place** in a lobby when you briefly switch to another app.
- **People's Choice in co-op**: the lounge, where three players pick units together.
- Smaller lobby improvements, such as a start countdown.

## Friends and profiles
- Friend and follow lists.
- Viewing other players' profiles.
- Lending a support unit to friends.

## Translation
Units, events and some menus added near the end of the game's life were only released in Japanese. Reflipped already sends English text for them. Next:
- **A human review** of every translated text.
- **The last Japanese messages** built into the app itself, such as the lock message and error suffix.
- **Japanese-only banners and images**, redrawn in English, starting with the most visible ones.
- New players getting mods and the translation with their first download, instead of one restart later.

## The app
- Testing and smoothing the build guide on Windows and macOS.
- The remaining in-game web pages (portal odds first) and menu entries.
- Downloads that keep going while the app is in the background.
- Phone notifications, such as a reminder when your stamina is full.
- **iPhone**: we are looking into whether an iPhone version is possible. Nothing is promised yet.

## Running a server
- Faster startup and loading for servers with many players.
- Health and usage information for server owners.
- Item names and icons in the admin website's mail form, instead of numbers.
- Hosting the game's data on cloud storage, for servers with many players.
- A tested guide for automatic HTTPS on a real public server.

## Not planned
- **Real-money purchases.** The paid bead shop stays closed; Reflipped will never take payments.
