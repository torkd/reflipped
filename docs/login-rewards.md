# Login rewards

The first login of each game day earns the next reward of the normal login calendar, and of any limited-time calendar that is open. Rewards arrive in the gift box: receive them there to add them to your inventory.

## How it works
- **The game day starts at 05:00 Japan time (20:00 UTC).**
- Missed days aren't made up later.
- The normal calendar starts over after its last entry; a limited-time calendar stops once all its entries are earned.
- In Accelerated mode, an event is shorter, so each day's first login earns that many entries of its limited calendar, up to the event's days so far: logging in every day still reaches the last entry.
- Reconnecting on the same game day gives nothing new, even if the calendar shows again.
- If the gift box is full, the rewards wait and arrive at a later login. An event ending doesn't take back gifts already earned.
- Importing a save keeps the server's record of earned gifts: an older save can't remove a newer reward or let one be received twice.

A new player sees the calendar right after the tutorial.

## Comeback and anniversary gifts
Some gifts are only for some players, as in the original game:
- **Welcome Back Login Perks**: a 10-day calendar of 150 beads a day (1,500 in all), for players who hadn't logged in since 1 December 2022.
- **Anniversary comeback calendar**: 5 days, for players who didn't log in between 1 March and 20 April 2024, new players included. **Anniversary presents** go to everyone else.
- **Welcome Back Gift**: for a player back after 60 days or more away, each time it happens.
- **Welcome Back Boost**: for the same returning players, 15 Boss Boosts a day instead of 3, for 7 days.
- **Welcome Back Portal**: for the same returning players, for 3 days, with its Star Sliver Exchange units and armaments.

With an event schedule, the dates above move with their campaign. Which of these a player gets is decided at their first login in the campaign, and an imported older save doesn't make a regular player look away.

**To give the comeback calendars to every player instead:**
1. On the admin website, open **Settings → Rewards**, card **Comeback login calendars**.
2. Choose **Everyone** instead of **As on the global server**, and press **Save comeback setting**.

## Periodic packs
The original sold monthly and rank packs for real money. Reflipped sells nothing, but you can give them to **every player** (never to some players only). Everything arrives in the gift box, and every bead is a free bead.

| Pack | At the start | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Pack | 500 beads and 30,000 mana | 50 beads at the first login of each day, for 30 days |
| 90-Day Monthly Pack | 1,000 beads | 100 beads a day, for 90 days |
| Staminade Monthly Pack | 110 beads | 1 Staminade Decanter a day, for 30 days |
| Rank Achievement Pack | 3,000 beads | 500 beads at ranks 10, 20, … 100 |
| Rank Achievement Pack II, III, IV | 3,000 beads each | 1,000 beads at every 5th rank: 105–125, 130–150, 155–175 |

**To turn them on:**
1. On the admin website, open **Settings → Rewards**, card **Periodic packs**.
2. Switch on the packs you want, and press **Save periodic packs**. Players get them at their next login.

Good to know:
- A monthly pack renews by itself while it stays on; days a player doesn't log in are missed, as in the original.
- A Rank Achievement pack pays each rank as the player reaches it, ranks reached before included.
- Turning a pack off starts no new term; a running one finishes.
- They work under Automatic reward rules too, since every player gets the same.
