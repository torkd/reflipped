Rotation

The windows the calendar runs through, in this order. A window is a range of whole game days (05:00 to 05:00 Japan time). Windows may overlap or go back in time; each time the clock goes back, daily and monthly resets and shop stock start over as on a new day. Summoning portals are open from {{contentStart}} to {{contentEnd}} UTC; a window needs one open on all of its days.

{{rotation}}

Events

The events in the game's data with the portals open during them: those that can be added first, newest first. Add one to put its game days at the end of the rotation, or "Go to" to set the server clock to its opening and test it on its own (this turns the calendar off).
