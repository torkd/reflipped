Every period in the catalog is included in automatic rotation. "Go to" moves the shared timeline to an event's opening, so one event can be tested (as a new run, with its own missions, scores, leaderboards and rewards); rank and kill rewards come during the event's results announcement, so jumping past it skips them; players on a personal period keep their offset, and every player sees the change after returning to the title. Adding a period to a Manual playlist brings along the banners, shops and other content scheduled on its historical dates. Some event types still need gameplay implementation.
